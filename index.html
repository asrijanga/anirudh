At Slack

I've spent twelve-plus years building software, and studied at San José State University. Today I'm a senior engineer at Slack, where my team and I built the generative-AI tooling Slack's own engineers use every day:

  • An escalation bot that reads help-channel posts, sorts them into six kinds of request, and answers from internal docs and history. It's live in 25+ channels and saved 3,000+ engineering hours in a year.
  • An AI assistant in Slack and an internal AI web app, with 4,000+ chat turns a month and 70%+ satisfaction.
  • A unified LLM platform: approved models behind one schema, knowledge-base APIs, a prompt playground and side-by-side model comparison, used by 16+ internal teams.

Altogether, an estimated 10,000+ engineering hours saved a year.
